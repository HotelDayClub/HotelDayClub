# Cancellation & Refund Policy
**Version [1.0] · Effective [date]** · Part of the Hotel Day Club Customer Terms.

**Your Australian Consumer Law rights come first.** Our services and the hotel's services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy limits them. If something goes seriously wrong (a major failure), you can choose a refund.

## At a glance
| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| You cancel **[24] hours or more** before your access hours start | Full refund to your card |
| You cancel **less than [24] hours** before, or don't turn up | No refund for change of mind. [Optional: free date change once, up to [2] hours before, subject to availability] |
| You booked **less than [24] hours** before your visit (same-day / next-day) | No free-cancellation window. We tell you this before you pay |
| The **hotel closes or limits** the facility you booked (weather, maintenance, event, safety, overbooking) | **Your choice:** full refund, or a free rebooking |
| You're **refused entry for a reason that isn't your fault** | **Your choice:** full refund, or a free rebooking |
| A facility listed in your pass isn't available, or the pass is **not as described** (e.g. "heated" pool not heated, spa closed) | A partial or full refund depending on how much of the pass you lost. For a major failure, a full refund if you choose |
| You're refused entry or asked to leave because **you broke the hotel's rules** shown at checkout (e.g. intoxication, unsafe behaviour, no ID, under age) | No refund, unless the law requires one |
| Add-ons (parking, gym, F&B credit, late access, upgrades) | Same rules as your pass. An unused F&B credit is [refunded if the visit is cancelled by the hotel; otherwise it expires at the end of your visit day] |
| Gift cards | Not refundable for change of mind. Valid for at least 3 years. If the hotel leaves Hotel Day Club, [we refund the unused balance / the hotel keeps honouring it] |

## Weather
- **Rain alone is not a closure.** Many hotels have indoor or covered pools, and each listing says whether the pool is indoor, outdoor, heated or unheated.
- If the **hotel** decides to close the pool or facility for weather, you get the choice above (refund or rebooking).
- [Optional per hotel: "Wet-weather flexibility": if the Bureau of Meteorology forecast at [7am] shows [x mm / thunderstorms], you may move your date for free.] Don't call it a "guarantee" unless it is one.

## How refunds work
- Refunds go back to the original card within [5–10] business days after we approve them. We don't charge a fee to refund.
- Cancel in your booking link or email [help@hoteldayclub.com.au] with your booking code.
- We decide refunds on the hotel's behalf, under our agreement with the hotel.
