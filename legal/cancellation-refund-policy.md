# Cancellation & Refund Policy
**Version 1.0 · Effective 12 November 2026** · Part of the Hotel Day Club [Customer Terms](/terms).

## 1. Your Australian Consumer Law rights
1.1 Our services and the hotel's services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law or any other law that cannot lawfully be excluded.
1.2 If something goes seriously wrong (a major failure), you can choose a refund.

## 2. At a glance
2.1 The table below summarises this policy. Clauses 3 to 5 set out the detail.

| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| You cancel 24 hours or more before your access hours start | Full refund to your card of the amount you paid |
| You cancel less than 24 hours before, or don't turn up | No refund for change of mind |
| You booked less than 24 hours before your visit (same-day / next-day) | No free-cancellation window. We tell you this before you pay |
| The hotel closes or limits the facility you booked (weather, maintenance, event, safety, overbooking) | Your choice: full refund of the amount you paid, or a free rebooking |
| You're refused entry for a reason that isn't your fault | Your choice: full refund of the amount you paid, or a free rebooking |
| A facility listed in your pass isn't available, or the pass is not as described (for example, a "heated" pool that is not heated, or a spa that is closed) | A partial or full refund depending on how much of the pass you lost. For a major failure, a full refund if you choose |
| You're refused entry or asked to leave because you broke the hotel's rules shown at checkout (for example, intoxication, unsafe behaviour, no ID, under age, or misusing a room number) | No refund, unless the law requires one |
| Add-ons (parking, gym, late access, upgrades) | Same rules as your pass |

2.2 If a listing offers a date-change or weather option for that hotel, the details are shown on the listing. This policy does not itself promise a free date change or a wet-weather date move.
2.3 Food and drink are not sold through Hotel Day Club. You buy and pay for them at the venue.

## 3. Weather
3.1 Rain alone is not a closure. Many hotels have indoor or covered pools, and each listing says whether the pool is indoor, outdoor, heated or unheated.
3.2 If the hotel decides to close the pool or facility for weather, you get the choice in clause 2.1 (refund or rebooking).
3.3 Any extra date-change or weather option is shown on the listing for that hotel.

## 4. How refunds work
4.1 Refunds go back to the original payment method after we approve them. Your bank or card issuer controls how long the credit takes to appear. We don't charge a fee to refund, and we do not deduct a card or payment-processing fee from any refund.
4.2 Cancel in your booking link or email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with your booking code.
4.3 We process refunds under this policy and the agreed Hotel Terms.
4.4 This applies to every refund: a guest free-cancellation refund, a partial refund, and a refund because the hotel closed, cancelled, or refused entry for a reason that is not your fault. You receive the amount you paid (or the partial amount, where a partial refund applies). We do not deduct a card or payment-processing fee from the refund.
