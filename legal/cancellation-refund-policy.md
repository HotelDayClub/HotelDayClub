**Draft.** This is not a final cancellation or refund policy.

## No charge

This demo does not take payment. Nothing is charged, so there is no refund to pay out and no card to reverse.

## Example cancellation line

Listings and the rules card say: free cancellation up to 24 hours before (hotel terms apply). That sentence is example copy for the fictional hotels. It does not create a right against a real hotel, because no real booking is made.

## Weather and closures

An example hotel can block a date. A blocked date cannot be booked in the demo. That is sample availability, not a promise from a real venue.
