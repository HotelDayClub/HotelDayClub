# Cancellation & Refund Policy
Part of the Hotel Day Club Customer Terms.

**Your Australian Consumer Law rights come first.** Our services and the hotel's services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in this policy limits them. If something goes seriously wrong (a major failure), you can choose a refund.

## At a glance
| What happened | What you get |
|---|---|
| You cancel **24 hours or more** before your access hours start | Full refund to your card |
| You cancel **less than 24 hours** before, or don't turn up | No refund for change of mind. A free date change may be available once, subject to availability |
| You booked **less than 24 hours** before your visit (same-day or next-day) | No free-cancellation window. We tell you this before you pay |
| The **hotel closes or limits** the facility you booked (weather, maintenance, event, safety, overbooking) | **Your choice:** full refund, or a free rebooking |
| You're **refused entry for a reason that isn't your fault** | **Your choice:** full refund, or a free rebooking |
| A facility listed in your pass isn't available, or the pass is **not as described** (for example, a "heated" pool that is not heated, or a closed spa) | A partial or full refund depending on how much of the pass you lost. For a major failure, a full refund if you choose |
| You're refused entry or asked to leave because **you broke the hotel's rules** shown at checkout (for example, intoxication, unsafe behaviour, no ID, under age) | No refund, unless the law requires one |
| Add-ons (parking, gym, food and drink credit, late access, upgrades) | Same rules as your pass. An unused food and drink credit expires at the end of your visit day. If the hotel cancels the visit, that credit is refunded |
| Gift cards | Not sold on this demo. If a gift card is offered later, it is not refundable for change of mind and is valid for at least 3 years |

## Weather
- **Rain alone is not a closure.** Many hotels have indoor or covered pools, and each listing says whether the pool is indoor, outdoor, heated or unheated.
- If the **hotel** decides to close the pool or facility for weather, you get the choice above (refund or rebooking).

## How refunds work
- Refunds go back to the original card. We don't charge a fee to refund.
- Cancel from your booking page and include your booking code.
- We decide refunds on the hotel's behalf, under our agreement with the hotel.

## Contact and complaints
If you're not happy with the outcome, you can contact NSW Fair Trading.
