# Hotel Day Club Privacy Policy
**Version 1.0 · Last updated 11 November 2026 · Effective 12 November 2026**

## 1. Who we are
1.1 Hotel Day Club is a registered business name (ABN {{ABN}}) ("we", "us").
1.2 We operate an online booking platform for day passes to hotel pools and spas. The hotel supplies the day pass and facilities and is the supplier. We take bookings and payments for the hotel through the platform. This policy explains how we handle personal information.
1.3 We assume the small business exemption in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) applies to us at present. We aim to handle personal information in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles as a matter of good practice. This policy does not say that we are bound by the APPs, and we have not opted in to the Privacy Act under s 6EA.
1.4 Customer information is held by the Hotel Day Club business, not as anyone's personal property. When a Pty Ltd is formed, that information will transfer to the company. It will also transfer if we sell or restructure the business. In each case it will still be handled under this policy (or an updated version we post). We will update clause 1.1 with the new entity's name and ABN (and any ACN) if that happens.

## 2. What we collect
2.1 **Booking details:** your name, email, mobile number, booking date, hotel, pass, add-ons, party size, arrival window if you give one, and whether you checked in.
2.2 **Payment details:** handled by our payment provider, Stripe, when live payments are switched on. We see only limited details (for example, card type, last 4 digits, and payment status). We don't store full card numbers.
2.3 **Acceptance and consent records:** when you accepted our Terms, the hotel's rules and the risk warning, when you consented to us sharing your details with the hotel for the booking, which version of this policy you saw, the time, and the IP address used.
2.4 **Communications:** emails, messages, complaints, reviews and survey answers you send us.
2.5 **Incident notes:** if the hotel reports an incident or refused entry linked to your booking, we record that an incident was reported and the hotel's reference, rather than injury detail, where possible. This information is kept separate from ordinary booking data, with access limited to people who need it to handle the incident, a complaint, a claim or a legal requirement. A note may still include health information if that is needed for those purposes.
2.6 **Marketing preferences:** whether you opted in to hot-day alerts and offers, and any unsubscribe.
2.7 **Website data:** device, browser, pages visited and approximate location from IP, through cookies and Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-9F5ZH1T4YN) on our public marketing pages. You can block cookies in your browser.
2.8 We don't collect or keep copies of your ID. Hotel staff only *look at* your photo ID at check-in.
2.9 If you book for other people, give us only the details we ask for, and tell them about this policy.
2.10 **Waitlist and notify-me sign-ups:** your email, and if you choose to give them, your first name, suburb or postcode, destination of interest, whether you are joining as a guest or a hotel, and a hotel name.
2.11 **Hotel or partner enquiry contacts:** if you enquire about a hotel partnership (including by choosing "I'm a hotel" on the waitlist, emailing us, or using a partner enquiry form), we collect your name, email, hotel name and the content of your enquiry, and any other details you choose to send.

## 3. How we collect it
3.1 We collect personal information:
3.1.1 directly from you, when you join a waitlist or notify-me list, enquire as a hotel or partner, make a booking, send us a message, leave a review or otherwise contact us;
3.1.2 from the person who booked for you, if someone else made the booking;
3.1.3 from the hotel, such as check-in status, no-show information, and incident reports (including a hotel reference);
3.1.4 from Stripe, about payment status and fraud-screening outcomes; and
3.1.5 automatically, through cookies and Google Analytics when you use our public website.

## 4. Why we collect it
4.1 We collect this information to take and confirm bookings and waitlist or enquiry requests, take payment, send booking and service messages (confirmations, reminders, changes, closures, and responses to enquiries), let the hotel check you in, process cancellations and refunds, deal with incidents, complaints and insurance claims, prevent fraud and misuse (for example, of referral credit), improve our service, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and, **only if you have opted in**, send marketing.

## 5. Children
5.1 We do not collect personal information directly from anyone under 18. The person who books must be 18 or over.
5.2 If children are included on a booking, we collect only a headcount or an age band for those children, not their names or other personal details.

## 6. Who we share it with
6.1 **The hotel you booked:** your name, email address, mobile number, booking code, pass, party size, add-ons, arrival window and any notes you add, so it can check you in, run the day safely, and contact you about this booking (for example, if you're running late, don't arrive, or there's an incident). You give a separate, express consent to this disclosure when you book. The marketing opt-in is a different box and is left unticked unless you choose it. We record that consent with the version of this policy, the time and the IP address. The hotel handles information under its own privacy policy and our agreement with it, which lets it use your details only for your booking. We do not sell your information or give it to hotels for their marketing.
6.2 **Service providers** who help us run the business, and who may use the information only to provide services to us:
6.2.1 payments: Stripe;
6.2.2 website hosting and the booking application: Render, using an SQLite database on a persistent disk;
6.2.3 website analytics: Google Analytics 4 (see clause 2.7);
6.2.4 business email for {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}: Google Workspace;
6.2.5 transactional email: Resend (we do not currently send SMS);
6.2.6 file backups: copies may be kept in Google Drive;
6.2.7 our accounting and tax advisers and the accounting software we use.
6.3 **People working in the business** (including any partner, staff member or contractor) may access personal information only as needed to do their work for us, and on confidential terms.
6.4 **Professional advisers and insurers** (lawyers, accountants, our insurer or broker) when needed, for example for a claim.
6.5 **Authorities** where the law requires or allows it (for example, police after a serious incident).
6.6 We do not run advertising pixels (for example, Meta Pixel or Google Ads remarketing) on checkout or booking-confirmation pages. Those pages in our booking application do not include advertising pixels or the Google Analytics tag. We found no advertising pixels in the site source we checked. Google Analytics runs on our public marketing pages (see clause 2.7).

## 7. How we hold and secure it
7.1 Booking and account records are held in the SQLite database on our Render-hosted application and its persistent disk. Payment card data is handled by Stripe, not stored by us as full card numbers. Business email is held by the email host in clause 6.2.4. Analytics data is held by Google. Backups may be downloaded and stored in Google Drive.
7.2 Access to admin and hotel tools is limited to people who need it. We use access controls, strong passwords and two-factor authentication on admin accounts, and encrypted connections.
7.3 Incident notes are stored separately from ordinary booking records and are available only to people who need them for the purposes in clause 2.5.
7.4 We use reasonable steps to protect personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 8. Overseas disclosure
8.1 Some of our providers store or process data outside Australia. Stripe may process data in the United States and India, and in other countries where Stripe or its service providers operate. Google Workspace, Google Analytics 4 and Google Drive may process data in the United States and in other countries where Google or its agents maintain facilities. Render may process data in the United States, Germany or Singapore (the regions Render publicly lists for its services) and in other countries where Render or its sub-processors operate. Resend sends our transactional email from Japan and stores and processes related customer data in the United States. These providers, and their sub-processors, may also operate in other countries. We choose providers with reasonable security and privacy commitments.

## 9. Marketing
9.1 We send marketing emails or SMS (for example, hot-day alerts, new hotels and offers) only if you have opted in through a separate box that is left unticked unless you choose it. Every marketing message identifies us as Hotel Day Club, includes a functional unsubscribe option, and shows a contact address ({{CONTACT_EMAIL}}). We act on unsubscribes within 5 working days.
9.2 Signing up to a waitlist or notify-me list is a request to be told when day passes are available near you (or, if you joined as a hotel, when we can talk about a partnership). That sign-up is your consent to those messages under the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth). It is not consent to unrelated marketing such as hot-day alerts and general offers, unless you separately opt in.
9.3 If you enquire as a hotel or partner, we may email you about that enquiry and about a possible partnership. Those messages identify us and include a way to ask us to stop. We treat a clear request to stop as an unsubscribe.
9.4 Booking messages and other messages needed to provide the service (confirmations, changes, refunds, check-in details) are not marketing, and you will still receive them after you unsubscribe from marketing.
9.5 After you unsubscribe, we keep a suppression record (your email or mobile and the date you unsubscribed) so we do not contact you again for marketing.

## 10. Referrals
10.1 If you share a referral link, **you** choose who to send it to. We don't email or text your friends unless they sign up themselves.

## 11. Reviews
11.1 After a visit we may invite you to review it. If we publish reviews, we show only your first name, the area and the date. We do not publish your email or mobile number.

## 12. Automated decisions
12.1 When live payments are switched on, Stripe may use Stripe Radar to screen payment attempts in real time for fraud. Stripe describes Radar as providing real-time fraud protection that screens payment attempts (including successful, declined, blocked and review-flagged attempts) and, depending on the settings, can allow a payment, block it, request extra authentication, or flag it for review (see https://docs.stripe.com/radar/how-radar-works). A blocked payment means that booking cannot be completed unless you try again or contact us.
12.2 We do not otherwise use computer programs to make, or to substantially assist, decisions that we consider would significantly affect you. If that changes, we will update this clause.

## 13. Retention
13.1 We keep booking, payment, refund and payout records for 5 years, which is the current tax record-keeping period while the business is a sole trader. Once the business is a Pty Ltd, we will keep those records for 7 years.
13.2 We keep incident records for as long as reasonably needed for any possible claim or insurer request.
13.3 We keep marketing contact details until you unsubscribe. We then keep the suppression record described in clause 9.5.
13.4 We delete or de-identify other personal information when we no longer need it for the purposes in this policy or to meet a legal obligation.
13.5 Our agreement with each hotel requires the hotel to delete guest lists within 30 days after the visit, unless the information is still needed for an incident.
13.6 We only state deletion timeframes that we will actually run as operational processes.

## 14. Data breaches
14.1 If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and recommend steps you can take, and we will notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires.

## 15. Access, correction and complaints
15.1 You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or to delete it where we don't need to keep it, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will respond within 30 days. There is no fee to ask.
15.2 If you have a privacy complaint, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner** at www.oaic.gov.au. The Commissioner's formal complaint powers apply to organisations covered by the Privacy Act.

16.1 We may update this policy and will post the new version here with its date.
