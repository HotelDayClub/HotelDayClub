# Hotel Day Club Privacy Policy
**Version [1.0] · Last updated [date]**

Hotel Day Club is a business name of Jason Baranov, ABN [x] ("we", "us"). We run an online booking service for day passes to hotel pools and spas, acting as booking agent for partner hotels. This policy explains how we handle personal information. We aim to follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth).

## 1. What we collect
- **Booking details:** your name, email, mobile number, booking date, hotel, pass, add-ons, party size, and whether you checked in.
- **Payment details:** handled by our payment provider [Stripe]. We see only limited details (for example, card type, last 4 digits, and payment status). We don't store full card numbers.
- **Acceptance records:** when you accepted our Terms, the hotel's rules and the risk warning, which version you saw, and the IP address used.
- **Communications:** emails, messages, complaints, reviews and survey answers you send us.
- **Incident notes:** if the hotel reports an incident or refused entry linked to your booking, a short note of what happened. This may occasionally include health information (for example, an injury), which we handle only as needed to respond to the incident, claims or insurers, or as the law requires.
- **Marketing preferences:** whether you opted in to hot-day alerts and offers.
- **Website data:** device, browser, pages visited and approximate location from IP, through cookies and analytics [name tool]. You can block cookies in your browser.
- **We don't** collect or keep copies of your ID. Hotel staff only *look at* your photo ID at check-in.
- If you book for other people, give us only the details we ask for, and tell them about this policy.

## 2. Why we collect it
To take and confirm bookings, take payment, send booking messages (confirmations, reminders, changes, closures), let the hotel check you in, process cancellations and refunds, deal with incidents, complaints and insurance claims, prevent fraud and misuse (for example, of referral credit), improve our service, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and, **only if you opt in**, send marketing.

## 3. Who we share it with
- **The hotel you booked:** your name, booking code, pass and party size (and any notes you add) so it can check you in and run the day safely. With your booking, you consent to this disclosure. The hotel handles information under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your information or give it to hotels for their marketing.
- **Service providers** who help us run the business: payments [Stripe], booking platform [SimplyBook.me / own system hosted on [provider]], email and SMS [provider], email hosting [Google Workspace / Zoho], analytics [provider], accounting [provider]. They may only use the information to provide services to us.
- **Professional advisers and insurers** (lawyers, accountants, our insurer or broker) when needed, for example for a claim.
- **Authorities** where the law requires or allows it (for example, police after a serious incident).

## 4. Overseas disclosure
Some of our providers store or process data outside Australia, including in [the United States, Singapore, the EU and other countries]. [List each provider and its location after checking.] We choose providers with reasonable security and privacy commitments.

## 5. Marketing
We send marketing emails or SMS (for example, hot-day alerts, new hotels and offers) only if you've opted in. Every marketing message identifies us and has an unsubscribe option, and we act on unsubscribes within 5 working days. Booking messages (confirmations, changes, refunds) aren't marketing and you'll still receive them.

## 6. Referrals
If you share a referral link, **you** choose who to send it to. We don't email or text your friends unless they sign up themselves.

## 7. Security and retention
We use reasonable steps to protect personal information: access controls, strong passwords and two-factor authentication on admin accounts, encrypted connections, and giving hotels only the minimum details. We keep booking and payment records for as long as needed for tax and legal purposes (generally [5–7] years). We delete or de-identify guest lists and marketing data when no longer needed (for example, marketing data within [24] months of your last booking or as soon as you unsubscribe and ask us to delete it). Hotels are asked to delete guest lists within [30] days after the visit.

## 8. Data breaches
If we become aware of a data breach that's likely to cause you serious harm, we'll tell you and recommend steps you can take, and we'll notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where the law requires.

## 9. Access, correction and complaints
You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or to delete it where we don't need to keep it, by emailing [privacy@hoteldayclub.com.au]. We'll respond within [30] days. There's no fee to ask. If you have a privacy complaint, email us and we'll respond within [30] days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner** at www.oaic.gov.au.
