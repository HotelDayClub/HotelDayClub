**Draft.** This is not a final privacy policy. Hotel Day Club is a demonstration site. Nothing here creates a relationship with a real hotel.

## What this demo collects

- Your name, email and mobile number, so the example hotel can see the booking and so the confirmation page can show it back to you.
- The date, seating, party size and any add-ons you choose.
- Whether you tick the optional marketing box. That box is separate from the booking and starts unticked.
- If you submit the hotel sign-up: the hotel name, neighbourhood, contact name, email, and the login you choose, so the application can be reviewed.

## Why

The booking details are stored so the example hotel’s guest list and check-in screen can show them. Contact details on a hotel sign-up are stored so an admin can approve or decline the example listing.

We do not take a card payment. The checkout is a mock.

## Marketing

We only record a yes for marketing email if you tick the box. Leaving it blank does not affect the booking or the sign-up.

## What this demo is not

Listings are examples. They are not real Sydney hotels, and sending the form does not make a booking or a partnership with a real hotel. Photos are stock images.

## Access

Demo hotel and admin logins are not published on the site. Passwords are set in the host’s environment, or generated when the server starts and written only to the server log.
