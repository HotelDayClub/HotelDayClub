## No real booking

Choosing a date and confirming does not reserve a place at a real hotel. No room, day pass or other service is supplied. No payment is taken.

## Example listings

Every hotel on the site is a fictional example. Names, photos, prices, hours and pool rules are sample content. They do not describe a real venue, and they are not a partnership with any Sydney hotel.

## Hotel sign-up

Submitting a hotel does not make you a partner and does not put a real hotel on sale. New sign-ups stay pending until an admin approves them inside this demo.

## Rules on a listing

Each example hotel has its own seating, hours and pool rules. Those come from that hotel’s settings. The hours on the listing, the rules card and the confirmation are the same hours. You tick the rules before a demo booking is saved.

## Marketing

The marketing checkbox is optional. It is not required to complete a demo booking or a sign-up.
