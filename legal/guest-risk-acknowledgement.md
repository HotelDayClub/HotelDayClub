It is modelled on the "risk warning" concept in s5M of the *Civil Liability Act 2002* (NSW), which a court may or may not apply to a particular hotel pool visit. A NSW lawyer must review it, and **each partner hotel must approve the facts and text for its own venue** (and its insurer may want to see it). This draft deliberately contains **no liability exclusion**. Whether to add a recreational services exclusion (CCA s139A / CLA s5N) is a decision for a lawyer. Items in [square brackets] are placeholders.
>
> Notes for the lawyer: s5L (obvious risks) only applies to "dangerous recreational activities" (significant risk of physical harm, s5K), and lounging or swimming at a hotel pool may not qualify. s5M warnings must be given in a manner reasonably likely to result in people being warned before they take part, and there are special rules for incapable persons (children). ## ⚠️ Risk warning
Swimming and using pools, spas, plunge pools, saunas and steam rooms carry **risks of injury, illness and death**, including:
- **Drowning or near-drowning**, including in shallow water, and especially for children and weak or tired swimmers.
- **Slips, trips and falls** on wet surfaces, stairs and pool edges. Spinal and head injury from **diving or jumping**. **Diving is not permitted** [unless the hotel says otherwise].
- **Heat-related illness, fainting and heart strain** in spas, saunas and steam rooms, and from **cold plunge** pools, particularly with alcohol, pregnancy, heart conditions, blood-pressure conditions or some medications.
- **Sunburn and heat stress** at outdoor pools.
- **Alcohol** greatly increases all these risks. Don't swim or use spas or saunas after drinking.
- Water-borne illness. Don't swim if you've had diarrhoea in the past [14] days.
