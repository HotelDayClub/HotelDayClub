# Risk warning and acknowledgement

Shown in full on the checkout page before you confirm, and repeated on your confirmation.

## Risk warning
Swimming and using pools, spas, plunge pools, saunas and steam rooms carry **risks of injury, illness and death**, including:
- **Drowning or near-drowning**, including in shallow water, and especially for children and weak or tired swimmers.
- **Slips, trips and falls** on wet surfaces, stairs and pool edges. Spinal and head injury from **diving or jumping**. **Diving is not permitted.**
- **Heat-related illness, fainting and heart strain** in spas, saunas and steam rooms, and from **cold plunge** pools, particularly with alcohol, pregnancy, heart conditions, blood-pressure conditions or some medications.
- **Sunburn and heat stress** at outdoor pools.
- **Alcohol** greatly increases all these risks. Don't swim or use spas or saunas after drinking.
- Water-borne illness. Don't swim if you've had diarrhoea recently.

**At the hotel you book:**
- The checkout page states whether a lifeguard is on duty, whether the pool is indoor or outdoor, and whether it is heated. Staff are not watching the pool continuously.
- It also states the hotel's age rules and children's hours. Every child must be actively supervised in the water by an adult on the booking.

## What you accept at checkout
Separate boxes, all required before you confirm:
1. **Risk warning.** You have read the warning. You understand that swimming and using spa facilities involve risks of injury and death, and whether a lifeguard is on duty. You decide if you, and anyone in your care, are fit to take part. You will not swim or use a spa or sauna after drinking.
2. **Hotel rules.** You have read the hotel's rules shown at checkout and will follow them.
3. **Terms.** You accept the [customer terms](/terms) and the [cancellation and refund policy](/refunds), and you have read the [privacy notice](/privacy). Nothing in these limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

You must be 18 or over to book. You will show this warning and the rules to everyone on your booking. An adult on the booking supervises each child.
