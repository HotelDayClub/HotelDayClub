# Risk warning and acknowledgement
**Version 1.0 · Effective 12 November 2026**

## 1. How this warning is shown
1.1 This warning is shown in full on the checkout page before payment (not hidden behind a link), repeated in the confirmation email, and on a sign at the pool entry if the hotel agrees.

## 2. Risk warning
2.1 Swimming and using pools, spas, plunge pools, saunas and steam rooms carry **risks of injury, illness and death**, including the risks in clauses 2.2 to 2.7.
2.2 **Drowning or near-drowning**, including in shallow water, and especially for children and weak or tired swimmers.
2.3 **Slips, trips and falls** on wet surfaces, stairs and pool edges. Spinal and head injury from **diving or jumping**. **Diving is not permitted** unless the hotel's listing or rules say otherwise.
2.4 **Heat-related illness, fainting and heart strain** in spas, saunas and steam rooms, and from **cold plunge** pools, particularly with alcohol, pregnancy, heart conditions, blood-pressure conditions or some medications.
2.5 **Sunburn and heat stress** at outdoor pools.
2.6 **Alcohol** greatly increases all these risks. Don't swim or use spas or saunas after drinking.
2.7 Water-borne illness. Don't swim if you've had diarrhoea recently.

## 3. At this venue
3.1 Whether a lifeguard is on duty is shown on the listing and at checkout for that hotel. Unless the listing says otherwise, no lifeguard is on duty. Staff are not watching the pool continuously.
3.2 The listing and checkout show whether the pool is indoor or outdoor, heated or unheated, and other venue facts for that hotel, such as depth.
3.3 Any spa, sauna, steam or plunge temperatures and time limits are shown on the listing or at the venue.
3.4 Age limits are set by each hotel and shown on the listing. Children on a booking must be actively supervised by a named adult on the same booking at all times.
3.5 Any other venue-specific hazards or rules are shown on the listing and at checkout.
